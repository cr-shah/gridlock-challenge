How Gridlock works

Gridlock compares only DESC × Georgia Power (GPC) planned projects — no other utilities, and never DESC-to-DESC or GPC-to-GPC.

Geography is primary. Every pair is measured by closest-point distance (Shapely, on a local flat projection) — not centroid-to-centroid — then placed into a distance tier:

  • Touching / crossing → Must coordinate
  • < 1.6 km → ROW / land coordination potential
  • < 8 km → Site logistics coordination potential
  • < 40 km → Crews / equipment coordination potential
  • ≥ 40 km → excluded from the results entirely

These tiers describe coordination potential based on distance — they are not a safety or risk severity rating.

Timeline overlap is secondary. It adds a small bonus to the ranking, but it can never outrank a closer geographic tier — two projects 1 km apart always rank above two projects 20 km apart, regardless of timing.

Unresolved geography is excluded, not guessed. A project without usable coordinates remains in the catalog but is never mapped, compared, or assigned a made-up location.

Verified and estimated geometry appear together in one coverage view and are always labeled. Estimates never replace official geometry. County anchors and single estimated points cannot create a touching, under-1.6 km, or under-8 km tier.

Evidence is public-source only. Every verified project traces back to a cited public planning document; nothing is scraped live or inferred without a source.

AI may assist with extraction and explanation; geographic overlap is verified with deterministic GIS calculations, not by a model's judgment.
